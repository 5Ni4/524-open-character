# Open decisions

## Current direction

- Welcome many interpretations and alternate settings for 524.
- Some community ideas may be adopted into official canon, with the project clearly marking what has been adopted.
- Make 524's official materials broadly reusable, including AI use.
- Keep creators' fanworks permission-based. Reuse of another person's fanwork requires that creator's permission.
- A creator may be able to grant the project advance permission for specific uses when submitting work for possible official adoption.

## To decide

- Final project and repository name.
- License for official character materials and reference assets.
- Required credit and unofficial-work labeling.
- Whether commercial use of official materials is unrestricted and how AI uses are described.
- Submission route for official-adoption candidates.
- Permission scopes for fanworks, including gallery display, official adoption/editing, AI use, and broader public reuse.
- How official status, alternate branches, and changes are versioned.
- Who reviews proposals and maintains the repository.
